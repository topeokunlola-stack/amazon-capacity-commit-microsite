Why committed capacity

Certainty

Each commitment is backed by a delivery date. Amazon releases new capacity only against signed demand, so promises are built to be kept.

Lower cost per unit

Workloads that qualify can move to custom chips. Amazon reports about 30% better price-performance for Trainium2 (company claim).

Clarity

One dashboard shows utilisation, delivery dates and cost per unit. Shoppers on Amazon.com see the all-in price and delivery date before checkout.

Who it is for

Built for

AI labs and large enterprises that plan compute years ahead and need capacity, price and dates they can rely on.

Not built for

Teams that need capacity created on speculation, with no commitment. They can still use Burst at list price.

The offer

Three clear tiers replace overlapping choices

Pick the commitment that matches how predictable your demand is. Every tier shows the same cost and delivery information.

Committed

3-year term, minimum use. Reserved capacity, locked price, delivery dates, and help moving workloads to custom chips.

Flexible

1-year term, lower minimum. Priority over burst capacity, with an option to step up to Committed.

Burst

No commitment. Use capacity when it is free, at list price. Availability is best-effort.

How it works

1. Commit

Share your compute plan and sign a term.

2. Plan

Get a delivery schedule and a migration plan for qualifying workloads.

3. Deliver

Capacity arrives on the promised dates; your dashboard tracks it.

4. Review

Quarterly review of use, cost per unit and step-up options.

For shoppers on Amazon.com

The same idea applies at checkout: the all-in price, including shipping and tax, and the delivery date appear before you enter your address details, so there are no late surprises.

Illustrative example: a workload that costs 100 units a month on-demand would cost about 60 units on Committed, and about 80 on Flexible, before any gain from moving to custom chips.

What sets the price

Commitment length

Longer terms let Amazon release capacity with confidence, so they earn a deeper discount.

Chip choice

Workloads that run on Trainium or Graviton can cost less per unit.

Minimum use

A higher minimum use commitment supports a lower price.
